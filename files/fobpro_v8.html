Tool comparison
  1. Autel KM100
    OBD key learning ~90% of vehicles, chip read/clone/write, IKEY generation. Best budget/beginner entry point. ~$429.
  2. VVDI Key Tool Max (Xhorse)
    Excellent chip cloner. Strong VW/Audi. ~$500.
  3. Lonsdor K518ISE
    Strong Toyota/Lexus and smart key. Good backup for vehicles where a budget tool hits a coverage limit. ~$800.
  4. Autel IM508S
    Full diagnostics + better BMW/Benz/VW coverage than entry-level tools. ~$1,500.
  5. Autel IM608 Pro II
    Industry standard. Widest coverage, EEPROM, dealer-level access. ~$2,800.
Sourcing — the four-rung ladder
Cheapest workable option first; climb only when the job demands it. This is how you keep 15 SKUs doing the work of 150.
  1. 1 · Universal programmable remotes (60-70% of jobs)
    Autel IKEY (pairs with your KM100) or Xhorse XS/XE/XN series (need an Xhorse tool to generate). One universal SKU covers dozens of models; Xhorse XS supports ID46/47/49/4A/4D/63/MQB48 and unlimited regeneration — a mis-generated key goes back in the bin. Carry 6-10.
  2. 2 · Model-specific quality aftermarket
    KeylessOption, Keyless2Go, Dorman — when the customer wants the factory look for less. Buy from locksmith distributors, not marketplace sellers (counterfeit boards are real).
  3. 3 · Refurbished OEM
    Original tested boards in fresh shells — true-OEM electronics at half the dealer price. Only from vendors who bench-test before shipping (most major distributors mark these clearly). NOT the same as a used eBay fob — those are usually VIN-locked.
  4. 4 · New OEM (Strattec / dealer)
    Strattec IS the OEM manufacturer for most GM/Ford/Stellantis keys — buy via distributors for true-OEM domestic at wholesale. Dealer parts counter for late-model (2023+) prox and anything 2025+ Toyota.

There's almost no formal schooling for this trade — everyone starts where you are. This is the researched, current (2026) path from zero to paid work, one checkbox at a time. Your progress saves on this device.
Pitfalls — other people's scars, your protocols

Lishi practice — reps to field-ready
Phase 3 skill: pick-and-decode locks to originate keys without codes. Log a rep for every clean bench pick. Nothing goes near a customer's lock until it's boring on the bench (25+ reps). Keyways below match this database's vehicles.
Keep learning — where the real knowledge lives
  • r/Locksmith and the Auto Locksmith Forum — real techs answering real job questions daily.
  • Tool-brand user groups (Autel / Lonsdor / Xhorse on Facebook) — coverage-update news lands here first, weeks before blogs.
