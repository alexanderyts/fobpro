Tool comparison
  1. Autel KM100
    OBD key learning ~90% of vehicles, chip read/clone/write, IKEY generation. Best budget/beginner entry point. ~$429.
  2. VVDI Key Tool Max (Xhorse)
    Excellent chip cloner. Strong VW/Audi. ~$500.
  3. Lonsdor K518ISE
    Strong Toyota/Lexus and smart key. Good backup for vehicles where a budget tool hits a coverage limit. ~$800.
  4. Autel IM508S
    Full diagnostics + better BMW/Benz/VW coverage than entry-level tools. ~$1,500.
  5. Autel IM608 Pro II
    Industry standard. Widest coverage, EEPROM, dealer-level access. ~$2,800.
